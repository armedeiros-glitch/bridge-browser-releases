Browser for delegated execution

Your AI thinks.
Bridge executes.

Bridge Browser is a minimal Chromium browser designed to let a compatible AI operate the web on your behalf while keeping control on your computer.

Private alpha · Windows
AI-agnostic by design

Bridge is the execution layer, not the intelligence layer. The architecture is designed so compatible AI systems can connect without turning the browser itself into an AI destination.

Local control stays local

Remote control can be paused from the desktop app. Consequential actions can require explicit approval on the computer before they execute.

Minimal by principle

No feed, news hub, shopping layer or attention trap. Bridge exists to move a task from AI intent to web execution, then get out of the way.

The bridge, not the destination.

The product goal is simple: install Bridge, link a compatible AI, ask for work, and let Bridge execute. The less time you need to spend managing the browser itself, the closer the product is to its purpose.
