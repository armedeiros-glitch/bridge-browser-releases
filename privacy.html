Trust & privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 8, 2026 · Private alpha

Short version: Bridge Browser is built to execute browser actions you request through a connected AI while keeping sensitive browser credentials on your computer. We collect only the account, device, command and operational data needed to run and secure the service.

1. What Bridge Browser is

Bridge Browser is a delegated web-execution browser. A compatible AI or controller can request browser actions, and the Bridge desktop app executes those actions on the user's computer subject to local controls and safety restrictions.

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Bridge Browser, we may process:

  • Account information: email address and authentication records used to sign in and link devices.
  • Device information: a device identifier, display name, app version, platform, connection status, last-seen timestamps and remote-control mode.
  • Linking information: records that associate a signed-in account with one or more Bridge Browser devices.
  • Command information: requested action type, parameters, status, timestamps, errors and returned results. Depending on the action you request, this may include URLs, page text, element metadata, screenshots or interaction details.
  • Security and operational information: records needed to detect failures, enforce permissions, expire commands, prevent unsafe replay and troubleshoot the service.

3. Sensitive browser data

Bridge Browser is designed to reduce exposure of sensitive data. Browser cookies and site sessions remain in the browser profile on the user's computer. The desktop app does not expose passwords, payment-card fields, one-time-password fields or file-input contents for remote typing. Page-reading tools mask form values and avoid returning editable-field contents.

However, information visible on a webpage can be returned when the user asks a connected AI to read the page or capture a screenshot. Users should avoid requesting page access to information they do not want processed by the connected AI or Bridge service.

4. How we use information

  • to authenticate accounts and link Bridge devices;
  • to deliver requested browser commands and return results;
  • to show device status and enforce remote-control settings;
  • to secure the command queue, prevent replay and investigate errors;
  • to improve reliability and safety during the private alpha.

5. Service providers

Bridge Browser currently uses infrastructure providers, including Supabase, for authentication, database and edge-function services, and GitHub for public software distribution and documentation. Connected AI providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies when you choose to connect them.

6. Retention

During the private alpha, account, device, command and operational records may be retained as needed to operate, debug and secure the service. Automated retention limits are still being finalized. You may request deletion of account-linked Bridge data through the support channel below.

7. Your controls

You can pause remote browser commands from the Bridge Browser desktop interface. You can also unlink or forget a device. Some consequential actions may require a local approval before execution.

8. Security

Device credentials are stored on the computer using platform-protected secure storage where available. Server-side credentials are stored as hashes where applicable. Database access controls and command-expiration rules are used to limit unauthorized access and stale execution.

9. Children's privacy

Bridge Browser is not intended for children under 13 and is not designed to knowingly collect information from children.

10. Changes

This policy may change as Bridge Browser moves beyond private alpha. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy requests, account-data questions or deletion requests, use the Bridge Browser support page.
