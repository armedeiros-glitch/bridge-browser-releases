Support

Need help with Bridge?

Bridge Browser is currently in private alpha. For installation problems, connection issues, privacy requests, account-data requests or bug reports, open a support issue in the public distribution repository.

Open a support request
Before reporting

Include the Bridge app version, operating system and what you expected to happen. Do not post passwords, tokens, payment data or private page content.

Privacy & deletion

For deletion or account-data requests, say that the issue is a privacy request. Do not include sensitive browser content in the public issue.
